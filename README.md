# Duo OS

**Duo OS** is an open-source operating system project by **thunder 3d inc** — a reimagining of Apple's platforms with a unified Duo design, built the Apple way: as an Xcode build, modelled on Apple's own implementations and checked side-by-side against stock behaviour.

Duo OS comes in two forms:

- **Duo OS** — for iPad (and iPhone Duo): Duo Split View, Stage Manager, Slide Over and Windowed Apps, desktop mode through Assistive Access, your choice of main apps, main AI, app store and files app — never forced.
- **Duo OS+** — for Mac: a modified version of macOS 27 or later that mixes macOS with StandBy mode and the Duo design, for the Mac mini.

## Duo OS (iPad / iPhone Duo)

- **Duo Split View** replaces iPad Split View — the divider can be locked at 50/50, and Duo OS can run the *same app* on both sides (real App Store apps, not web views)
- **Stage Manager**, **Slide Over** and **Windowed Apps** all stay, as their own modes
- **Desktop mode is Assistive Access**: plug into a monitor or TV by cable and it enters Assistive Access automatically with your keyboard and mouse; unplug and you're back in Duo OS, setup saved
- **Your choices, never forced**: stay or upgrade; Apple App Store or your own store; your main apps, main GPS, main files app (Finder ships, Files doesn't); side-button main AI — Apple Classic Mode, Siri, or your AI of choice
- Light and dark mode, Always On Display, Adaptive Power on every device, Safari with no tab limit, CarPlay and CarPlay Ultra
- Runs Mac, iPad and iPhone apps — all of them — plus APK compatibility

## Duo OS+ (Mac)

- A **modified version of macOS 27 or later** — macOS mixed with StandBy mode and the Duo design
- **StandBy instead of screensavers**: flick the cursor and StandBy turns off and lets you unlock the Mac
- **Control Center combines both** — the macOS Control Center and the iOS 27 Control Center in one
- Brings the **iOS / iPadOS App Library**; the macOS App Library is discontinued
- **Stage Manager** from macOS and iPadOS
- **Finder** is the files app — no iOS/iPadOS Files app
- **Fully customizable** — anyone can customize Duo OS+ how they want
- Light and dark mode
- Delivered as an **OS installer app**, the way Apple ships its own macOS installers

## Status

In active development. Current target: a Duo OS installer ready before **May 2027**, ahead of the Mac mini it will run on.
